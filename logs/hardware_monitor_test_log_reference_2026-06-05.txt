FAST DECISION RULES
------------------------------------------------------------
PASS:
- TBPRESS ENTER/UPDATE/EXIT cycle completes per stress burst.
- jit_est_us peak then decay behavior is visible.
- Backlog now (back_now / tb_cross_backlog_now) returns to 0.

- No drop activity during obvious overload (strict-drop policy not taking effect).
- Persistent backlog with no recovery.
- High jitter estimate sustained after stress has ended.
